Why terminals are still here

By Ada Example · September 1, 2026

Fifty years after the first video terminals, text is still how a great deal of work gets done, from servers and build systems to the agents that now browse on our behalf.

Text is small, fast and searchable. A page of it loads in an instant, travels well over a slow link, and can be read by a person, a script, or a model, without translation.

Terminals also changed. They draw colors, pictures, and even graphics, with protocols like Sixel and Kitty's, so a text-first tool no longer means a picture-free one.

What didn't change is the promise: the same words, however they are shown, and the same keys, whoever is at them. That is why the terminal, like the command line, keeps coming back.
